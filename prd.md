## 1. Executive Summary & Goal

*   **Lead Generation:** Driving qualified demo bookings and inquiries.
*   **Trust Building:** Establishing credibility and authority for enterprise and institutional buyers.
*   **Customer Acquisition:** Designing clear conversion pathways for standard and enterprise clients.
*   **Market Positioning:** Positioning Mealiez as India's leading mess management and food operations platform.

## 6. Interactive Lead Generation Tools

### 6.1 ROI Calculator (Wastage & Savings)
*   **Purpose:** Allow prospective buyers to calculate standard annual cost savings by digitizing food operations.
*   **Inputs:**
    1.  *Number of members/customers* (slider/numeric input)
    2.  *Average cost per meal (INR)* (slider/numeric input)
    3.  *Current estimated food wastage percentage* (slider/percentage input, e.g., 10% - 40%)
*   **Calculations:**
    *   $\text{Daily Meal Value} = \text{Members} \times \text{Average Cost Per Meal}$
    *   $\text{Annual Meal Cost} = \text{Daily Meal Value} \times 365$
    *   $\text{Current Food Waste Cost} = \text{Annual Meal Cost} \times \text{Wastage Percentage}$
    *   $\text{Estimated Savings} = \text{Current Food Waste Cost} \times 0.60$ (assuming a 60% reduction in wastage with Mealiez optimization)
*   **Output:** Interactive display of *Estimated Annual Savings (INR)* with a direct "Claim Your Savings - Book a Demo" CTA.

### 6.2 Cost Leakage Calculator (Attendance & Billing Mismatch)
*   **Purpose:** Quantify the financial loss due to unauthorized diners, billing mismatch, or manual tracking errors.
*   **Inputs:**
    1.  *Daily meals served* (numeric input)
    2.  *Average meal price (INR)* (numeric input)
    3.  *Estimated attendance mismatch percentage* (default 5% - 15%, slider input)
*   **Calculations:**
    *   $\text{Daily Leakage} = \text{Daily Meals Served} \times \text{Average Meal Price} \times \text{Mismatch Percentage}$
    *   $\text{Annual Leakage} = \text{Daily Leakage} \times 365$
*   **Output:** Interactive presentation of *Estimated Annual Revenue Leakage (INR)* with a direct CTA: "Plug Your Revenue Leaks - Connect with an Expert".

### 6.3 Multi-Step Demo Booking Funnel
*   **Step 1: Organization Type** (Hostel, College/University, Industrial Canteen, Corporate, Cloud Kitchen, Subscription Mess, Other)
*   **Step 2: Number of Members/Customers** (< 100, 100 - 500, 500 - 2000, 2000+)
*   **Step 3: Existing Challenges** (Select all: High Food Wastage, Manual Billing/Ledgers, Attendance Discrepancies, Inventory Theft/Loss, Poor Member App Experience, Other)
*   **Step 4: Contact Details** (Full Name, Work Email, Phone Number, Organization/Company Name, Designation)
*   **Post-Submission:** Immediate confirmation, automated email follow-up, and redirection to a calendar booking widget (e.g., Cal.com / Calendly) for real-time scheduling.
*   **CRM Readiness:** Standardized webhook payloads matching Hubspot, Salesforce, or Zoho format.

## 7. Technical & Non-Functional Requirements

### 7.1 Performance Requirements
*   **LCP (Largest Contentful Paint):** < 2.5 seconds.
*   **CLS (Cumulative Layout Shift):** < 0.1.
*   **INP (Interaction to Next Paint):** < 200ms.
*   **Mobile-First Design:** Fully responsive templates optimized for viewports from 320px up to ultra-wide desktop monitors.
*   **Optimization Strategies:** Automatic image compression (WebP/AVIF), asset minification, component lazy loading, and modern caching strategies.

### 7.2 SEO Requirements
*   **Dynamic Metadata:** Automated title tag, description, and canonical URL generations for all dynamic routes (products, solutions, and blog posts).
*   **JSON-LD Schema Markup:** Structured data implementation:
    *   `Organization` schema for the parent brand.
    *   `Product` schema for module pages.
    *   `SoftwareApplication` schema for the SaaS offerings.
    *   `FAQPage` schema for the FAQs.
    *   `Article` schema for blogs.
*   **Sitemap & Robots:** Automatic XML sitemap generation containing priority routes and dynamic URLs. Custom `robots.txt` mapping.
*   **Programmatic SEO support:** Scalable routing framework to generate region-based or industry-specific landing pages programmatically.
*   **OpenGraph & Twitter Cards:** Configured meta tags and standardized templates for high-impact social sharing.

### 7.3 Analytics & Tracking Setup
*   **Google Analytics 4 (GA4):** Global tag implementation with standard page view tracking.
*   **Microsoft Clarity / Hotjar:** Session recording and heatmaps to monitor scroll depth and interactive calculator usage.
*   **PostHog / Mixpanel:** Detailed event tracking for high-intent actions.
*   **Tracked Actions:**
    *   CTA Clicks (Header, Hero, Pricing, Footer)
    *   Form Abandonment (tracking which step of the multi-step form is dropped)
    *   Successful Demo Bookings
    *   Interactive Calculator Usage (capturing input ranges to understand lead profiles)
    *   Pricing Page Tier Toggles & Comparison Interactions
    *   Industry Page Conversions

### 7.4 Security & Compliance
*   **Encryption:** Strict HTTPS implementation with secure SSL certificates and HSTS headers.
*   **Rate Limiting:** IP-based limiters on API endpoints and form submissions to prevent spam attacks and automated bot bookings.
*   **Spam Protection:** Captcha integration (e.g., Cloudflare Turnstile or Google reCAPTCHA v3) on all public form elements.
*   **Validation:** Strict client-side and server-side data validation of all inputs (email patterns, phone validation).
*   **Privacy & GDPR:** GDPR-ready cookie consent banners, explicit privacy policy updates, and secure storage of lead data.
